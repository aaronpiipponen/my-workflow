---
name: Plan phase remediation
description: Use when a phase landed but failed verification, or its contract turned out wrong. Re-details the same phase in place instead of opening a new one or lowering the bar.
---

Read `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS/PLANNING/PLAN-PHASE-REMEDIATION.md` before re-detailing a failed phase.

Keep the parts that passed, fix the steps at full grade, and leave the status row `active`
until the retest passes. Do not split the phase to make it fit, weaken an assertion, or turn a
failure into a smaller success.
